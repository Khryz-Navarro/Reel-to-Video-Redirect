# Reel to Video Redirect

A lightweight Google Chrome extension that automatically redirects Reels to the standard video player interface, giving you back full video playback controls (progress bar, scrubbing, volume slider, and playback speed).

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Automatic Redirection**: Automatically detects when a Reel URL is opened and redirects it to the normal desktop video player.
- **Full Video Controls**: Enjoy standard playback controls (scrubbing/timeline bar, full-screen, picture-in-picture, volume adjustments).
- **Lightweight & Fast**: Zero unnecessary background overhead; runs seamlessly as you browse.
- **Privacy First**: Operates entirely locally in your browser. No personal data or browsing history is tracked or transmitted.

---

## 🛠️ Installation (Developer Mode)

Since this extension is loaded locally:

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/` in the address bar.
3. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select the `Reel to Video Redirect` folder.
6. The extension is now installed and active!

---

## 📖 How It Works

When navigating to a Reel URL, the extension intercepts the request and transforms the link into the standard video URL format, redirecting your browser seamlessly without requiring manual copy-pasting.
